Redefining healthy productivity.

Being productive doesn’t mean spending as many hours as possible in front of a screen.

It means knowing when to focus.
Knowing when to pause.
And giving yourself the chance to come back feeling better.

Meet ReMemo.

The app that reminds you to take breaks.

ReMemo is built for people who spend a big part of their day in front of a screen. But unlike a simple timer, ReMemo can base your reminders on your actual screen time, so breaks follow how much you have really been using your device, not just the clock.
