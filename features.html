ReMemo Features

Take better breaks, your way.

ReMemo is designed to make healthy breaks easier to remember, and easier to stick to. Choose how you want to be reminded, personalize your experience, and let ReMemo take care of the rest.

Smart screen-time reminders

Your screen time becomes your reminder.

Most break timers simply count down from a fixed interval. ReMemo can do something different. Set how much screen time you want before your next break, and ReMemo reminds you when you reach it.

If you take a break before the timer is up, the timer resets automatically and starts counting again once you come back to your device.

You can also choose clock-based reminders if that works better for your routine.

Your screen time. Your limits. Your breaks.

Eye breaks

Eye break reminders

Spending long hours in front of the computer can strain your eyes, leading to headaches, eye pain, blurred vision and fatigue.

ReMemo reminds you to look away and focus on something far away. This short break helps keep your eyesight healthy and comfortable throughout the day.

Hydration

Don’t forget the water sitting next to you.

When you’re focused, drinking water can be the last thing on your mind.

ReMemo reminds you to stay hydrated throughout your workday.

Stretch & move

You’ve been sitting long enough.

Long periods at your desk can leave you feeling stiff and uncomfortable.

ReMemo reminds you to get up, stretch, move your shoulders, loosen your neck, or simply step away from your desk. A few seconds of movement can be enough to break the cycle.

On your computer

Desktop version

Your screen time shouldn’t stop at your phone.

ReMemo is now on your computer too. It brings your reminders directly to your desktop while you work, so you can stay focused without losing track of how long you’ve been in front of your screen.

Get it on Microsoft Store
Take the break

No more “Skip break”

Sometimes we know we should take a break… and still press Skip.

Now you can turn the skip button off in Settings, so a break can only be finished. Because ReMemo isn’t here to help you work through every reminder. It’s here to help you actually stop.

Personalization

Make ReMemo yours.

Your reminders should feel like you.

Customize the experience to match your personality. Choose your characters, backgrounds and sounds to make every reminder feel more personal. You can also choose between ReMemo’s colourful default interface or switch to a minimalist style for a more subtle experience.

Your reminders. Your style. Your ReMemo.

Languages

ReMemo speaks your language.

The whole app is translated into six languages, from the reminder screens to the Collection. Choose yours in Settings; Arabic switches the entire layout right to left.
