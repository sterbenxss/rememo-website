About

A Message from the Creator

Hi, I am Petra, a UI/UX designer. For years, I have designed digital products for clients and redesigned existing apps to make them more intuitive, accessible, and enjoyable to use. While I loved helping bring other people’s ideas to life, I always dreamed of creating and publishing an app of my own.

The biggest challenge was deciding what to build. Technology evolves so quickly that it often feels as though every useful idea already exists. I wanted to create something meaningful, something that could genuinely improve people’s daily lives rather than simply add another app to their phones.

Then I remembered something that happened during my university years.

One morning I woke up with such intense eye pain that I could barely open my eyes. I honestly thought I was going blind. After visiting a doctor, I learned that the cause was severe eye strain from spending too many hours in front of a computer. Thankfully it was nothing permanent, but it taught me an important lesson about how easily we neglect our own wellbeing when we become absorbed in our work.

That experience stayed with me for years.

Today many of us spend most of our day in front of screens. Whether we work in an office, study, design, program, write, or simply spend long hours using our devices, it is surprisingly easy to forget to drink water, stretch, or even look away from the screen for a few moments. Over time those small habits can contribute to eye strain, headaches, dehydration, neck and back pain, poor posture, and fatigue.

I wanted to build something that would quietly support people throughout their day. My goal was not to interrupt productivity but to encourage healthier habits through gentle reminders that fit naturally into everyday life.

That is how ReMemo was born. ReMemo reminds you to drink water, stretch, and rest your eyes with customizable reminders and smart screen time features. Every design decision was made with simplicity, clarity, and user experience in mind because I believe healthy habits should be easy to maintain.

Thank you for choosing ReMemo. I truly hope it helps you feel healthier, more comfortable, and more productive every day.

Special thanks to my husband, Aymen, who helped me build this app and supported me throughout its development. I would also like to thank his sister, Khawla, for generously sharing her development tools with me and helping make this project possible.
